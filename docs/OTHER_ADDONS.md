# What other translation addons have (25 Nov 2026)

Checked: ForeverAR (Arabic, Forever, v0.5.21), UALL Forever (Ukrainian, v1.0.18), Forever WoWPoPolsku 2.0 (Polish, v0.6.1),
WoWTR (Turkish, Forever v26.1106), WOW-UKRAINE (Classic/Retail), Viet Craft (Vietnamese, alpha), WoWForeverIT (Italian, v0.9.15).
Sources: their CurseForge pages/changelogs; PolishForever and WoWLang on GitHub. Hooks are mostly not published (inferred).

| # | Feature we lack | Who has it | Effort | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | NPC gossip / greeting dialogue + NPC speech bubbles and yells | ForeverAR, UALL, Polish, WoWTR, Ukraine, Viet | L (code + data) | very high |
| 2 | Native quest log + objective tracker in Persian (not only our pane) | UALL, Polish, Ukraine, Italian | M | very high |
| 3 | Item tooltips (names, stats, use/equip, flavour) | ForeverAR, UALL, Polish, WoWTR, Ukraine, Italian | M-L | very high |
| 4 | Books, letters, plaques (ItemText frame) | ForeverAR, UALL, WoWTR, Ukraine | M | high |
| 5 | Untranslated capture/export with id + context (slash command, SavedVariables) | ForeverAR, Polish, Ukraine, Italian | M | very high (feeds our data) |
| 6 | Show English original on Shift / hover | UALL (Shift), Polish, WoWTR (toggle) | S-M | high |
| 7 | Questie / Immersion / DialogueUI integration | Polish, ForeverAR (Questie) | M each | high |
| 8 | Cinematic / intro subtitles | WoWTR, Polish, Ukraine | M | high (story) |
| 9 | Tutorials / help tips | WoWTR, Ukraine | S-M | medium |
| 10 | Broad UI frames (character, bags, mail, AH, group finder, guild) | UALL, ForeverAR, Polish, Ukraine, Italian | L | high |
| 11 | Factions, achievements, PvP ranks | UALL, ForeverAR | M-L | medium |
| 12 | Presets (full / story / quests / off), minimap menu, auto-pause in combat or raids | ForeverAR, Ukraine | S-M | medium |
| 13 | Translated guide steps (RestedXP) | ForeverAR | M-L | high for guide users |
| 14 | Voice-over / TTS | UALL voice pack, Polish + Spoken | L | high but costly |

Suggested order for WoWFA: 5 (capture, also fixes our coverage), 6 (Shift original), 1 (gossip + bubbles),
2 (native tracker/log), 4 (books), 3 (items, after the item bank), 7, 8.
